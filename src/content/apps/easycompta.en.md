## Your obligations, without the effort

The **income ledger** carries the entries expected under the French
micro-entreprise regime: date, client, nature of the operation, amount, method
of payment and invoice reference. The **purchase register** records your
suppliers, your expense categories and your notes.

Entry is deliberately quick: clients, suppliers, categories and payment methods
are remembered as you go, so a routine income entry takes seconds. Search is
instant across every entry, grouped by month.

## Steer the business, not just the turnover

This is the difference that matters day to day: the dashboard does not only show
what you collected, but your **estimated net profit after contributions**.

- Yearly, quarterly or monthly view
- Chart of the last twelve months
- Turnover to declare, category by category

## URSSAF without surprises

EasyCompta estimates the contributions due according to your activity type:

- **sale of goods**
- **commercial or artisanal services** (BIC)
- **other services and liberal activity** (BNC)

Rates are adjustable in the settings, which lets the figures match your real
situation — ACRE relief, a change of scale. The amount to declare is therefore
already prepared when your monthly or quarterly declaration comes round.

## Your data stays with you

Everything is stored **locally, on your device**. No sign-up, no accounting data
sent to a server, no bank connection to authorise. The encrypted backup takes
one gesture and restores onto a new device in seconds. Light or dark theme, and
an interface entirely in French.

## A French regime, described in English

Worth being explicit: EasyCompta serves one specific legal framework, the French
micro-entreprise, and the app itself speaks French. This page exists in English
so that a French-speaking micro-entrepreneur living abroad — or anyone weighing
the app up before installing — can judge what it does without guessing.

## EasyCompta Premium

The trial is free for 3 days, ad-free and without a bank card. The subscription
then keeps:

- advanced analytics and statistics — breakdown by category, client, supplier,
  VAT and payment method;
- multi-year history and a global view of the business;
- quarterly and monthly periods, on top of the yearly view;
- export and sharing of your ledgers in PDF and CSV;
- photo or PDF receipts attached to each entry, included in backups;
- no advertising.

The free version shows a banner on the home screen. Keeping your income ledger
and your purchase register stays entirely free and unlimited.
