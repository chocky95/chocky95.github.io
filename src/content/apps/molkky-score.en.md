## Keep score at Mölkky without pen and paper

Scoring Mölkky is simple on paper and annoying in practice: one fallen pin
scores its number, several fallen pins score their count, and everyone
forgets the drop back to 25.

Mölkky Score takes care of it.

### Solo, group, or team games

Every player for themselves or in teams, with a configurable target score —
50 points for a classic game, 30 for a quick one.

### The drop back to 25, applied automatically

If a player goes over the target, their score drops back to 25. It's the most
common scoring mistake at the end of a game, and you never have to think
about it again.

### The three-miss rule

Three consecutive throws without touching a pin eliminate the player — or
reset their score to zero, depending on your settings. The app tracks the
miss count for each player.

### Winning sets

A match can be played over several sets. The game ends as soon as a player or
team wins the required number of sets.

## Run a Mölkky tournament in minutes

Two modes, depending on the format you want for your tournament.

### Leisure mode: the knockout bracket

No limit on players or teams. The app builds a bracket sized to however many
entrants you register. The winner of each match moves on to the next round.

### Official mode: pool phase, then Gold and Silver brackets

A complete setup, built for a real club or company tournament:

1. **Pool phase** — entrants are split into pools, with a number of courts and
   a distribution you choose. Each pool plays a fixed number of sets.
2. **Final brackets** — once pools are done, players are sorted into a
   **Gold bracket** or a **Silver bracket** based on their ranking, each with
   its own knockout format.

### Bronze match, final, and podium

Semi-final losers play a bronze-medal match. The final decides gold and
silver.

### Random team generator

Handy when nobody wants to be the one picking teams.

## ELO ranking and player profile

ELO ranking doesn't just count wins — it weighs how strong your opponent was:
beating a beginner earns little, beating the group's champion sends you
flying up the ranks.

Every player gets a profile with:

- a **Precision, Toughness, Composure, Power, Form** radar chart;
- their **Nemesis** and **Easy prey** — the opponent who beats them most
  often, and the one they dominate;
- their personal records and progress curve.

## All your Mölkky stats

Games played, podiums, eliminations, total throws, points per throw. Enough
to settle any post-game argument with numbers.

## Share the score live during the game

The app publishes the game or tournament to a private web page, updated in
real time: scores, sets, throws, pools and bracket. You share it by
**QR code**, **link**, or a **printable poster** — useful for a club or
company tournament, where spectators follow along on their phone.

**Data is automatically deleted 48 hours after the game ends.** Nothing stays
online.

## Export and archive your games

- Tournament sheet, match table and bracket in **PDF**
- Full game history and tournament summary
- Backup and restore your players and history in JSON format
- Player cards with photo, taken with the camera or picked from the gallery

## The rules of Mölkky, right in the app

The full rules and the pin-placement diagram are built in: the goal, setting
up the 12 pins, the throwing line at 3 to 4 metres, scoring, the three-miss
rule, going over 50. No more digging through the box for the rulebook.

## What devices does Mölkky Score run on?

The app is available on **Android**, at version 1.0.21. It's translated into
nine languages, including **Finnish**, **Swedish** and **Estonian** — Mölkky
is a Finnish game, and that's where it's played the most. Light, dark, or
system theme.
