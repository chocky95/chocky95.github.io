## The real Papayoo, digitised

The goal is to **survive**: score as few points as possible. Dodge the
Payoos, which cost their point value, and above all the **Papayoo**, worth
40 points on its own.

The full rules are built into the app: the goal, card values, the card
exchange at the start of each hand, the die roll that sets the Payoo suit,
then trick-taking play.

## Play solo, or with your friends

### Solo mode against bots

Four levels: **Easy**, **Medium**, **Hard** and **Expert**. Easy forgives
beginner mistakes, Hard counts cards.

Higher levels aren't unlocked right away: they open up as you progress, and
**Expert** requires three wins at Hard. Higher difficulties also earn more
XP.

### Online multiplayer

Create a game or join one, invite your friends, share your profile by QR
code. Pending games are visible from the home screen.

## Jokers and special powers

Unlocked as you progress:

- **Trick history** — review what's already been played
- **AI hint** — a suggestion for the best move
- **Played card stats** — from level 15
- **Sonar** — reveals who holds the Papayoo, level 20
- **Defense** — brings the Papayoo from 40 down to 20 points, level 25
- **Hand swap** — level 25

## Leaderboards and progression

A **monthly** leaderboard and an **ELO** ranking, tracked separately for
solo and multiplayer, with a **Champion of the Month** badge for the top
players. There's also a full game history, a player profile, and
achievements to unlock.

## Shop, customisation and quests

Customisable card decks, weekly gold, quests to claim. Gold is also earned
from the **Payout Pyramid**, the daily **Joker Wheel**, or rewarded videos.

## Papayoo Premium

A **one-time purchase**, not a subscription:

- no more ads;
- all jokers free, unlimited;
- permanent benefits.

## Which devices can you play on?

The app is available on **Android**, at version 1.0.178, and translated
into **fifteen languages**.
