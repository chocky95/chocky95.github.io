## A garage, boosters, a collection

Everything starts in the **Garage**. A free booster refills there every two
hours, two at most in reserve, and a **welcome booster** is waiting on first
launch with a guaranteed Gold card or better. Each booster holds **six
cards**: five ordinary cards and one rare card, never Standard. The draw is
made by the server, and the opening is a small ceremony: the pack tears open,
the cards flip one by one, the best one in pole position.

## Real cars, ranked from D to S

The catalogue gathers cars that really exist, from city cars to hypercars.
Each one carries a **class**, which sets its rarity and its base value:

| Class | What it covers | Chance per card |
|---|---|---|
| D — Everyday | City cars, family cars, vans | 40% |
| C — Family | Hot hatches, affordable roadsters | 30% |
| B — Premium | Sports cars, GTs | 18% |
| A — Supercar | Supercars, icons | 9% |
| S — Legend | Hypercars, legends, museum pieces | 3% |

Every card shows the car's key figures — power, 0 to 100 km/h, top speed,
year, weight — and a fun fact. Photos come from Wikimedia Commons, with their
author and licence listed in the "Photo credits" screen.

## Six editions, from Standard to Prestige

A card's second dimension is its **edition**, in other words its finish. It
multiplies the car's value:

| Edition | Multiplier | What sets it apart |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | ×1 | White frame |
| Silver | ×3 | Reflection |
| Gold | ×10 | Numbered |
| Platinum | ×40 | Numbered, holo |
| Diamond | ×200 | Numbered, particles |
| Prestige | ×1,000 | Numbered and capped at 50 copies per car |

From **Gold** up, every card drawn from a booster carries a copy number,
printed on the card and kept when it changes hands. A **guarantee** watches
over dry spells: with no Platinum for fifteen boosters, the next rare card is
at least Platinum; with no Diamond for sixty, it is at least Diamond. And one
booster in two thousand is a **god pack**, every card Gold or better.

## Fuse, recycle, craft

Duplicates are never wasted:

- **fusing** two identical copies yields one copy of the next edition up;
- **recycling** a Standard, Silver or Gold returns dust and coins;
- **crafting** a card you are missing costs dust, once a day.

A card marked as a favourite is protected: it can never be recycled, fused,
sold or traded by mistake.

## Albums and collections

The collection reads like a numbered binder, where every empty slot shows the
car that is missing. **Albums** group cards by make, country, decade and
category; completing one pays coins, and make albums pay gems. **Themed
series** arrive through time-limited campaigns: once a campaign ends, its
cards can only be found on the market or made by fusion.

## The market and trades

Every card has an **estimated value** in coins, recomputed nightly from its
class, its edition and the number of copies in circulation. On the
**market**, you list a card at the price of your choice, between half and
three times that value, for one, three or seven days; a tax is taken on each
sale. The "Recent", "Bargains" and "Priciest" views refresh every minute.

**Direct trades** happen between friends, cards for cards, with trade tokens
renewed every week. The game works out the value on each side and refuses a
trade that is too lopsided: trades exist to complete albums, not to move
value around.

## Missions, login streak, levels

Three **missions** a day and three a week, a seven-day **login streak** with
coins, gems and bonus boosters, permanent **achievements**, and **levels**
that gradually unlock recycling, fusion, the market and then trades. A daily
**leaderboard** lists the hundred most valuable collections.

## Friends by QR code

Every player has a QR code and an invitation link. Scan a friend's code, or
open their link, and you are friends: you can look at their collection once a
day, propose a trade, and find them in your profile. The camera is used only
to read the QR code; the image is analysed on the phone.

## Premium Pass, gems and ads

The game is free and stays free. Three sources fund it, all optional:

- **rewarded videos**, which you choose to watch to shorten the timer of the
  next booster or earn a few coins — never a banner, never an interstitial;
- **gems**, sold in packs with the first purchase doubled, which buy Premium
  boosters (the last two cards go up one edition, and the last one has a one
  in ten chance of being a card from the limited, numbered Premium
  collection) or coins;
- the **Premium Pass**, a monthly subscription: ten simultaneous listings
  instead of five, five free boosters in reserve instead of two, two crafts a
  day, six showcase slots, one extra mission a day and a week, fifty gems
  every month and a badge.

Nothing is exclusive to paying players except cosmetics and speed: a free
player can complete any album.
