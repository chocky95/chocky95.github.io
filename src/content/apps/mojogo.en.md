## Two games, one app

That's what makes Mojogo distinctive: **Mojo** and **Jungo** are two
different games, with their own rules, their own leaderboards and their own
achievements. Each game's rules are available anytime from the home menu.

## Mojo

### The principle

Score **as few points as possible**. The round ends the moment a player
hits 50 points, and the lowest score wins.

### The cards

78 cards in five colours:

| Colour | Values |
|---|---|
| Blue | 0 – 1 |
| Green | 2 – 4 |
| Yellow | 5 – 6 – 7 |
| Orange | 8 – 9 – 10 |
| Red | 11 – 12 |

Plus the **Mojo card**, which indicates the direction of play.

### How a turn plays out

- **lower** card than the previous one: your turn ends;
- **equal** card: you play again;
- **higher** card: you draw a card.

### Mojo Time and being Countered

**Mojo Time** kicks in as soon as you're down to three cards or fewer in
hand. Being **Countered** costs a 10-point penalty and reverses the
direction of play — enough to flip a game's ending. A **2 Discards**
variant changes the pace even further.

## Jungo

A **combination** game: the goal is to build Jungos. It has its own
jokers:

- **Best combo AI** — the engine's suggestion
- **Oracle** — reveals the remaining cards, 1 to 8
- **Vision** — the next card in the draw pile
- **Shield** — four points off
- **Reload** — a full hand, replaced

Over **fifteen achievements** to unlock: First Jungo, King of the Jungle,
Perfect Round, Invincible, Combo Master.

## Solo or online

**Solo**, against bots, from easy to hard. **Online**, with invitations, a
friends list, a multiplayer hub and profile sharing by QR code.

The **monthly leaderboard** is tracked separately for each game and mode:
Mojo solo, Mojo multiplayer, Jungo solo, Jungo multiplayer.

## Progress and customisation

Shop, customisable decks, quests and achievements. Gold is earned from the
daily **Joker Wheel** or from rewarded videos. An **AI assist** can suggest
the best move while you're learning.

## Premium

A **one-time purchase**, not a subscription: no more ads, all jokers
unlimited, permanent benefits.
